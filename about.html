About Oreone

Development solutions for a changing Africa.

We work with businesses, institutions and development partners to solve practical problems, strengthen systems and create sustainable opportunities.

Who we are

Focused on the problem. Committed to practical outcomes.

Oreone International Limited is a development and business transformation organisation. We bring together business understanding, development practice, research, partnerships and technology where these capabilities can improve outcomes.

Our work connects enterprise growth with institutional effectiveness and the wider economic systems that shape opportunity.

Our mission

Turn important challenges into practical solutions.

We identify real needs, build relevant responses and work with partners to deliver, learn and improve.

Our vision

More opportunity. Stronger systems. Sustainable progress.

We want to contribute to productive businesses, capable institutions and economic ecosystems where people and organisations can thrive.
