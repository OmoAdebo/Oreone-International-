About Oreone

Development solutions for a changing Africa.

Oreone works with businesses, government institutions and development organisations to address practical challenges across enterprise development, finance, institutional strengthening, business transformation and technology.

The Oreone Story

Real challenges. Practical solutions.

Oreone was founded on a simple idea: important problems create opportunities for meaningful businesses and stronger institutions.

We bring together people, expertise, technology and partnerships to develop practical solutions that create value, build resilience and unlock long-term growth.
